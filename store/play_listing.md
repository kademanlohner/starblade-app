## Full description

StarBlade Mission

StarBlade helps every kid grow a little braver, kinder, and more capable each day, without shame and without needing to be perfect.

How we do it, for each kid:

Love: Every kid starts every day as a hero. Stars are earned and never taken away.

Structure: Small, clear quests, one step at a time, so kids learn that showing up is what builds strength.

Trust: Kids keep their promises to themselves, and parents cheer the effort, not just the result.

Freedom: Kids choose their path and their quests within limits parents set.

Repair: A missed day isn't a failure. Kids learn to try again, fix it, and keep going.

Our promise to parents: high warmth and high expectations. StarBlade never uses fear, ranks one kid against another, or makes a kid feel small, and it gives parents a clear way to notice and celebrate real growth.

StarBlade: small quests, real growth, never stop growing.

What kids do

- Math quests: times tables, word problems, place value, and fractions
- Reading: passages, reading together, word practice, and a reading log
- Writing: build a story and keep it in a story book
- Drawing on a pad with a finger or a stylus
- A world map of short country visits
- A pet dragon that hatches and grows, and does not shrink
- Stars and days played that only go up

No ads. No sign-in. Progress stays on the device.
